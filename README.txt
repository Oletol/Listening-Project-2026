Cherdak — Listening Project 2026 website framework

Open index.html in a modern browser. Keep the assets folder beside it so the uploaded Telegram channel icon appears correctly.

The prototype includes responsive 15-week navigation, a project-book profile, weekly audio/discussion/activity/reflection slots, a working custom audio player with an MP3 download button, listening-stage tabs, and checklist progress stored in the browser.

Week 1 begins with a pre-listening 12-card phonetics study deck. It presents one term at a time, with Russian translations, definitions, examples, shuffle and review controls, and locally saved known/learning progress. After the recording, 15 transcript-checked discussion questions are organised into five expandable themes.

The 15 open questions are labelled for ungraded in-class discussion. A separate independent self-check automatically assesses a five-item concept–example matching task and a seven-question multiple-choice listening quiz, with instant feedback, explanations, shuffled answer order, and a locally saved best score.

The interface uses a readability-focused type scale across body copy, navigation, activities, assessment controls, and supporting labels. The earlier generic Language Toolkit section has been removed so the page stays focused on the weekly listening sequence.

Week 2 is connected to Part 1 — Vowels. It includes 14 pre-listening flashcards, 15 transcript-based classroom discussion questions in five themes, and an eight-question automatically assessed multiple-choice quiz with shuffled options, explanations, and a locally saved best score.

Weeks 4 and 5 follow the same pattern: 12 pre-listening flashcards, transcript-based in-class discussion questions in four themes (13 for Week 4, 14 for Week 5), a ten-question automatically assessed multiple-choice quiz, and a theory-and-practice block with two sound profiles, a spelling map, two automatically checked practice tasks, and a final speaking pass.
